GroundWork — RAG Quality Engineering Platform

GroundWork helps enterprises deploying AI assistants over internal knowledge bases solve hallucinations, misattributed citations, and confidence-eroding "almost right" answers. Measure, tune, and trust your retrieval quality.

What You Get

  • Tunable chunk profiles by corpus type (legal, support, engineering).
  • Precision and grounding attribution metrics.
  • Regression testing for retrieval quality over time.

Common Use Cases

Legal teams ensuring accurate citations, customer support platforms improving AI responses, and compliance teams auditing retrieval accuracy.

Demo the Platform

Explore the three-panel interface:

  • Corpus Manager: Upload and tag document libraries with pre-tuned chunk profiles.
  • Retrieval Quality Dashboard: Visualize precision scores, adjust retrieval weights, and track grounding confidence.
  • Chat + Attribution: Ask questions and see sentence-level grounding with confidence badges.
