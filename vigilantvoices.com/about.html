Our Story

We Founded Model Signal to End the Single-Model Trap

In 2024, our founders watched teams waste thousands of dollars per month routing every AI request through a single overpriced model — even simple tasks that a lightweight model could handle just as well. We built Model Signal to fix that.

Vigilant Voices is our flagship platform: a real-time AI mesh that scores your request for complexity, domain, latency need, and cost budget, then dispatches it to the model most likely to nail it. It's not just a router — it's an intelligent orchestration layer with output auditing, fallback chains, and deep observability.

Leadership: Randall Scott Avenell, Founder, Chaos Champion, and Justin Joseph Silva, VP, Mission Control.

Our Mission

Solve Today. Empower What Comes Next.

Our mission is to solve the highest-impact enterprise problems in front of us right now while building systems that make tomorrow safer, smarter, and more equitable. We measure success by one standard: when our work is done, organizations operate better and the world is better for it.

Full Observability

Every model call is logged with latency, cost, token count, and quality score. Nothing is hidden — you always know what ran and why.

Security First

Data never trains third-party models without your consent. PII scrubbing, audit logs, and role-based access are built in from day one.

Speed at Scale

Our mesh layer adds less than 20ms overhead per request. Routing intelligence should be invisible — and in Vigilant Voices, it is.
