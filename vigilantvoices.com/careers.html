Make Your Own Position

This is not a traditional job posting. It is an open invitation to design your role and build meaningful impact with us.

Our Mission

We solve critical enterprise problems now while engineering the systems, intelligence, and trust models needed for the challenges still to come. Vigilant Voices exists to help organizations make confident decisions in an AI-first world, today and in the future.

The Impact You'll Make

This is not a traditional job posting; it is an open invitation. We are looking for trailblazers who see opportunities others miss, identify a gap or bold new direction, and take ownership of making it real.

Whether you are a strategist, builder, creative, technologist, or something entirely unique, you will have the chance to design your own role and directly influence our growth, culture, and future.

What You'll Do

  • Identify a challenge, opportunity, or untapped potential within our business.
  • Propose and design a role that leverages your skills to address it.
  • Collaborate with leadership to refine your vision and set measurable goals.
  • Take ownership of your projects from concept to execution.
  • Continuously adapt your role as the company evolves.

What You'll Bring

  • A clear vision for how you can add value to our company.
  • A track record of turning ideas into tangible results.
  • Strong self-motivation and the ability to work independently.
  • Excellent communication skills to pitch, refine, and execute your ideas.
  • A willingness to take risks, learn quickly, and adapt.

Why You'll Love It Here

  • The freedom to shape your own career path with no cookie-cutter role definitions.
  • A collaborative environment where your ideas are heard and acted on.
  • Competitive salary and benefits tailored to your role scope.
  • Annual innovation budget to fund projects and experiments.
  • Flexible work arrangements and a culture that values creativity over conformity.

We are an equal opportunity employer and celebrate diversity. The best teams are built from a wide range of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills.

Enterprise Mission Statement Ideas

Idea 1: Build secure, intelligent systems that solve today’s highest-risk enterprise problems while preparing organizations for the unknown challenges of tomorrow.
Idea 2: Transform enterprise AI from experimental to dependable by combining automation, security, and accountability at every decision point.
Idea 3: Help every organization operate with confidence in real time by unifying identity, threat response, and data intelligence for both present and future threats.
