California Medicare FAQ

Answers to common Medicare questions from beneficiaries and families across California.

Most people first enroll around age 65 during their Initial Enrollment Period. Special Enrollment Periods can apply after qualifying life events, and annual windows are available for plan changes.

Medicare Advantage provides bundled coverage through private plans and networks. Medicare Supplement works with Original Medicare to help reduce out-of-pocket costs.

If you do not have creditable drug coverage, a Part D plan may help prevent late penalties and lower medication costs.

Networks differ by plan. We verify providers, facilities, and medications before you make a final enrollment choice.
