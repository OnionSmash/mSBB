Security Intelligence

Your AI-Powered First Line of Defense

Stack Lume goes beyond AI routing. Our Identity Intelligence and SIEM-integrated agents continuously watch your environment — detecting stale accounts, anomalous access, and insider threats before they become incidents.

IAM & PAM Agent

Autonomous Identity Hygiene

Most breaches don't start with malware — they start with a forgotten service account or an employee who left six months ago. Stack Lume deploys an always-on IAM/PAM agent that continuously audits your identity landscape and acts autonomously.

Stale Account Detection

Flag and quarantine accounts with no activity over a configurable threshold. Human review queued automatically.

System Account Audit

Enumerate all non-human identities — service accounts, API keys, machine tokens — and surface over-privileged access.

Privileged Access Review

Continuous PAM sweep validates that admin and privileged roles are justified, time-boxed, and actively monitored.

Autonomous First Response

Agent disables, quarantines, or escalates anomalous identities in real time — not after a ticket is opened.

Explore IAM Agent

Continuous Discovery

Every 15 minutes the agent re-scans your directory, cloud IAM, and SaaS connectors for new identities, role changes, and dormant accounts.

Risk Scoring Engine

Each identity is scored across age, last login, privilege level, and peer-group deviation. High-risk accounts trigger immediate workflow actions.

Compliance-Ready Reporting

Auto-generated access review reports map to SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and NIST 800-53 controls — ready for auditors on demand.

SIEM Integration

The AI Agent at the Front of Your SIEM

Most SIEM platforms drown analysts in noise. Stack Lume acts as an intelligent pre-processor — triaging, correlating, and responding to signals before they ever reach a human queue.

Signal Triage

AI agent receives raw SIEM events, classifies threat type, severity, and asset criticality — reducing analyst alert queues by up to 80%.

Behavioral Correlation

Correlates identity events, network activity, and data access patterns to detect multi-stage attack chains invisible to rule-based SIEM.

Autonomous Response

For confirmed high-confidence threats, the agent executes first-response playbooks — isolating accounts, blocking tokens, and creating incidents automatically.

Full Audit Trail

Every autonomous action is logged with reasoning, confidence score, and rollback instructions — full explainability for compliance and post-mortems.

Alert Channels

Alerts Where Your Team Already Works

Security incidents don't wait for email. Stack Lume routes critical alerts to the channels your team lives in — with full context, severity, and one-click remediation links baked into every message.

  • SMS Alerts

    Carrier-grade SMS for critical P0/P1 events. Configurable escalation chains with acknowledgement tracking.

  • Slack Integration

    Rich alert blocks with entity details, risk score, and inline Approve / Quarantine / Dismiss actions — no context switching required.

  • Microsoft Teams

    Adaptive Cards delivered to SOC channels with full incident payload. Reply to the card to trigger remediation workflows.
